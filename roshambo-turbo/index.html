HOW TO PLAY
THE CHANTEvery exchange is called out loud: ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS, SHOOT! Throw on SHOOT. Win the throw and your fighter lands the hit: rock punches, paper chops, scissors kick. TIMINGThrow right on the SHOOT beat for PERFECT TIMING and 30% more damage. Throw too late and you eat the hit. TELLSWatch your rival's glove on SCISSORS. Early opponents flash the shape they are about to throw. Later ones bluff. COMBOSEvery win in a row hits harder. Your signature throw does 25% more. SUPERWins and hits fill the meter. When it is full, arm SUPER before SHOOT. If you win the throw it becomes a giant projectile hand. If you lose, half the meter is gone. THE LADDERBeat the other seven fighters, then Sgt. Stone, Col. Kane and ShamboCorp's own Adelaide Mercer. Every rival has a habit. The scouting report tells you what it is. KEYSA S D (or J K L, 1 2 3, ← ↓ →) for rock, paper, scissors. SPACE arms super. P pauses, M mutes. Gamepad: X Y B and A.
