## 2026-10-28

- **Add Roshambo Turbo, a rock-paper-scissors arcade fighter** `60d8690`
  Rock-paper-scissors played as a 2.5D three.js fighter after Street Fighter II. Every exchange is chanted (rock, paper, scissors, shoot) while giant gloves pump on the beat; the winner of the throw lands a punch, chop or kick. Perfect timing on SHOOT, tells in the rival's glove, combos, signature throws and an armable super add skill on top of the guess.
