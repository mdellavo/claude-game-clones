## 2026-10-01

- **Add arcade assists to Claws Over Tarowa** `b5dc6f4`
  Lining up the next strafing run was too hard for most players. With arcade assists on, which is the default and can be switched off on the title screen, the plane breaks off after each pass or as soon as an arm winds up at you, extends, climbs, banks round and dives the nose onto a gill before handing the last stretch back. A label under the sight shows what it is doing, and any real stick input takes control immediately. A light aim magnet eases the nose onto a nearby weak point, and a low-altitude save pulls up before the water or the island.

## 2026-09-30

- **Add Claws Over Tarowa, a WW2 dogfight against a giant monster** `e815ed4`
  A three.js dogfight set on a Pacific atoll in 1944. The player flies a Warhawk with three AI wingmen against Lizzie, a monster wading across the lagoon toward the airfield, and loses if she reaches the beach.
