## 2026-11-06

- **Add Updraft Isle, a hang-gliding game over a tropical island** `c805cbf`
  Launch off a hilltop deck and glide a resort island in a bright, toy-like Wii Sports Resort style. Updrafts show as shimmering columns with rising sparkles, circling birds and a cumulus on top; circle inside one to climb while the vario beeps, or ride ridge lift where the trade wind meets the slopes. Three courses (Lagoon Hop, Ridge Runner, Grand Crossing) thread rings across the island and the open sea and end on a beach bullseye, scored on rings, time, accuracy and how softly you touch down.
