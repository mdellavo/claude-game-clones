How to fly

Steer← → or A D bank the glider. Steeper banks turn tighter but sink faster. Speed↑ / W pushes the bar out for speed (you dive). ↓ / S slows you down and stretches your glide. Hold ↓ just above the sand to flare. UpdraftsWarm air rises under the puffy clouds. Look for the shimmering columns, sparkles and circling seabirds, then turn tight circles inside them to climb. The vario beeps faster the harder you rise. Ridge liftThe trade wind blows from the east. Where it hits a slope it rises, so skim the windward side of the hills. RingsFly through the rings for bonus points. The arrow up top always points to the next ring or the finish. FinishLand on the bullseye on the beach. Closer to the centre and softer touchdowns score more. If you touch down anywhere else, the flight is over. KeysC camera · P pause · M mute · gamepad left stick. On a phone, drag the stick.
